Jumbleberry Fields cheat sheet

Every rule here comes from the exact solver and matches perfect play.

Perfect play averages 121.8 · best odds of 121+ 58% · 141+ 11%

1 · Pick your goal

Most Moonberries plays for 121 first, then takes risks for 141 once 121 looks safe. If you pick one fixed goal, pick 121.

2 · The par sheet

  • Each roll, chase the box you can most likely beat par in.
  • After the last roll, score where points − par is highest.
  • A bad round goes where it's least below par.

Played this way: 119.8 average, 98% of perfect.

3 · If you must take a zero

Points a round-1 zero costs you, cheapest first. Basket of 5 is the throw-away box; never waste Free Roll.

4 · Locking

  • Lock Moonberries (96% of the time while their box is open).
  • Lock Pickleberries, especially while their box is open.
  • Never lock a Fly.
  • Re-roll pairs of Jumble or Sugar; keep three (62%) or four (91%).
  • Round 1: don't chase a Mixed Basket. With JSPMF lock just PM.
  • One Moonberry beats three cheap berries early: JJJSM → lock just M.
  • Use all three rolls; perfect play stops early in only 11% of rounds.

5 · Scoring

  • Three Moonberries go in Moonberry: MMMJJ is 21 there, beating 25 in Basket of 3.
  • Five Jumble or Sugar go in Basket of 5: the only time it pays.
  • Don't spend Basket of 3 on a small hand. Save it for a big total (it averages about 21).
  • Take Mixed Basket and Basket of 3 when a good one comes; they're usually filled first.
  • Jumbleberry and Sugarberry aren't dump boxes. Perfect play zeroes them 0.4% of the time.
