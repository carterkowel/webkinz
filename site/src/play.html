Bramble Dice

Roll five dice up to three times a round, hold the ones you want, and fill all nine boxes. Score 121 for a star, 141 for two. Turn on the coach to see the mathematically best move, and check your review after each game.

How to play
  • Each die shows a red Redcurrant (2 points), yellow Cloudberry (2), green Gooseberry (4), purple Mulberry (7) or a black Beetle (0). Mulberries are rare: about 1 roll in 10.
  • Each round: press Roll, tap dice to hold them, roll again up to twice more, then tap a box. You can also score after the first or second roll.
  • Fruit boxes score that fruit only. Three/Four/Five alike need that many matching dice (Beetles count as a match) and score the total of all dice. Medley needs one of each fruit. Free pick always scores the total.
  • Every box is used once; a box can take a zero. Nine rounds, then your game is reviewed against perfect play.
  • Trainer mode (top of the game) quizzes you on real situations from perfectly played games and shows what each choice costs.
  • Your stats stay on this device. Finish a game with the coach off to add it to the shared leaderboard: Top scores, or Sharpest games for the fewest points given away.
