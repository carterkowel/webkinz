Jumbleberry Fields helper

Use your phone as a live guide

  1. Open Jumbleberry Fields on your computer.
  2. Tap Start the camera and allow it.
  3. Turn your phone sideways and prop it up facing the screen, with the whole game in view: the jars, the SCORE, the scorecard and the x3 sign next to ROLL. It shows which jars to lock, when to press ROLL and which box to click, and keeps score by itself. You can start mid-game.

Playing for the most Moonberries over time. To change that, pick another goal in the helper below.

The camera picture is read on your phone and never uploaded or saved.

Keep this open on your phone while you play in Webkinz. Tap in your berries and it tells you exactly which jars to lock or which box to fill, using the same perfect-play math as the Jumbleberry Fields, Solved guide.

How to use it
  1. Pick a goal. Most Moonberries wins the most prizes over time. If you'd rather pick one target, play for 1 Moonberry: over many games it earns about 38% more Moonberries than always chasing 2.
  2. Roll in Webkinz, then tap the five berries in the helper, left to right. Or let your phone's camera do it: press Live camera, turn your phone sideways and point it at your game screen with the whole game in view. It marks which jars to lock and won't say ROLL until they're locked, highlights the box to click and its points, and keeps the scorecard by itself, reading the SCORE and the x3 roll counter from the screen. You can start it mid-game. Or press Read jars from a photo to snap one picture; check the jars match and tap any jar to fix it.
  3. Do what it says. If it says to lock jars, lock them in the game, roll, then press I locked them and rolled and enter only the new berries.
  4. When it says to score, fill that box in the game and press I scored it. The helper keeps the scorecard for you.
  5. Your game in progress is saved on this device, so you can close the page and come back.
